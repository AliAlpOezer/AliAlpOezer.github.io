I arrived in Germany on an Erasmus year having learned German at school in Turkey, started
at B2, and reached C2 within twelve months. Then I completed an entire Bachelor's degree in
that language.

The thesis was a **Dynamic Vehicle Routing Problem** simulation for warehouse logistics,
built in Python on SimPy and NetworkX. The interesting result was a look-ahead scheduling
heuristic that accounts for future tasks and came out roughly 10% more efficient than a
greedy baseline with no loss of solution quality. Graded 1.7.
