I started at **ODTÜ**, Turkey's most selective state technical university, admitted through
the national entrance examination where I placed in the top 0.15% nationally. The English
preparation year there is where I learned English properly.

I then moved to **İTÜ** in Istanbul to study economics, because most Turkish companies are
headquartered there and Ankara was not going to serve the career I wanted. Markets and
ethics, Rawls and Kant and Amartya Sen, and a political philosophy course I still think
about.

Neither programme was completed. I came to Germany in 2021 and started again.
